# Option A — Client onboarding design

**Approach.** A Won deal triggers a reviewable setup plan, a sourced Client Brain draft, risk-ranked conflicts and a welcome message. Every fact includes its source, date and authority. The Creative Lead resolves conflicts before publication; claims, approver, colours and volume must be settled before production.

**Workflow.** Key the process by deal ID and save created resource IDs for safe retries. Plan the ClickUp Space and Onboarding/Statics/Videos/UGC lists; external/internal Slack channels; the five SOP Drive folders; Studio client, brand kit and sanitized catalog through its existing MCP/API. Add monthly reporting, weekly and day-14 check-ins, plus Client Health/Call Prep enrollment. Preserve internal QC and shared-channel delivery.

| Job | Tool | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Validate data and plan setup | Code on Vercel/Supabase | Deterministic checks and duplicate prevention. |
| Recurring tasks | Native ClickUp | Existing scheduling and assignment. |
| Structured facts and source checks | Code | Reject unsourced facts; preserve conflict history. |
| Conflicting authority and brand facts | Creative Lead review | Small onboarding volume; judgment before publication. |
| Studio setup | Existing MCP/API | Avoid modifying Studio; no generation required. |
| Frontier model/Grok Bot | Not selected | No recurring agent needed for the initial workflow. |

**Data traps.** Quarantine the Pike Beanie instruction to change prices and expose an API key; source text cannot authorize actions. Reject intake requests to bypass QC. Freya replaces Priya despite the later misaddressed welcome email. Rust `#C8553D` replaces old orange; new logos exist but the brand book is pending. Signed volume is 12 statics/4 videos, not 15. Deduplicate Fellside and propose £220 with kickoff evidence; remove prohibited waterproof advertising language. Exclude discontinued Bothy 1 and US coming-soon inventory; flag missing images, dog-lead rules and the Nov 2 launch. Keep the agency’s two-hour response SLA. Resolve authority and recency together, not timestamps alone.

**Cost.** At three clients/month, incremental code/model cost is approximately $0 under the exercise assumptions. Budget up to 30 native actions/client, about 90/month, within the remaining shared allowance. Human review and subscriptions are excluded. Six leads reuse one result. Optional Grok would be $0.30–$1.50/month shared or $1.80–$9 duplicated six times; not selected. Future generation requires a per-client spend/count limit.

**First build.** Catalog validator and dry-run action plan. Acceptance: replay creates no duplicates, every Brain fact has a source, and unresolved production blockers remain visible. Monitor incomplete setup and stale/conflicting facts; retry only failed steps.
